ALGORITHMIC ARCHITECTURE

The 4-Step ANTU LSS PRO System Workflow

Every trade signal follows a strict, mathematical 4-stage sequential state machine inside the Pine Script.

01

Dynamic Liquidity Tracking

The script computes structural pivot highs and lows using ta.pivothigh() and ta.pivotlow() with configurable swing lengths (3 to 50 bars). Un-swept Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL) and Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL) are maintained in real-time memory arrays.

Memory Array Trimming
02

Liquidity Sweep Confirmation

When price penetrates a tracked liquidity level during the bar, but closes back inside the structural range (low < lvl and close > lvl for SSL, or high > lvl and close < lvl for BSL), a valid sweep is confirmed. On-chart labels "SSL▲" or "BSL▼" mark the event.

Bar Close Locked • Non-Repainting
03

Fair Value Gap Displacement

A sweep alone does not trigger a trade. The indicator monitors subsequent candles for institutional displacement: a 3-bar Fair Value Gap (Bullish: low > high[2]; Bearish: high < low[2]) that prints after the sweep within the expiration window.

3-Candle Imbalance Engine
04

50% Midpoint Retest & Risk Boxes

When price retraces to test the 50% midpoint (Consequent Encroachment) of the FVG, the setup executes. The script draws a dashed entry line, an ATR-buffered Stop-Loss box, and a Take-Profit box dynamically projected at 2.0x your risk distance.

Visual TP / SL Boxes • 2.0R Fixed Multiplier
